Privacy Policy

Towing J&J LLC · Oviedo, Florida, USA · Last updated October 20, 2026

What this app is

Towing J&J uses this app and its WhatsApp assistant only inside the company. The WhatsApp assistant talks only with the company's owner and its drivers. It never sends messages to customers or to anyone outside the team.

Information we keep

  • Team members: name and phone number, to recognize who is writing to the assistant.
  • Job records: customer name and phone, pickup and drop-off addresses, vehicle details and VIN, photos of the vehicle, prices, payments and expenses.
  • Messages and voice notes the team sends to the assistant, used only to carry out the request.

How we use it

Only to run the towing business: dispatch jobs, record payments and expenses, and prepare invoices and reports. We do not sell or share this information for advertising.

Service providers

The data is stored on our server (Render). Messages travel through the WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta). Text and voice notes may be processed by Google Gemini to understand the request.

Your choices

Anyone can ask Towing J&J LLC to see, correct or delete their information by contacting the company directly.
